2. Collected Data and Purpose of Processing

GPTalk is an AI-supported platform that provides all members of the University of Hohenheim with uncomplicated access to generative Artificial Intelligence (AI). The aim is to promote the integration of AI into work, teaching, and research processes as well as to create a space for exchange, reflection, and collaboration.

The platform enables access to various AI models, including OpenAI (ChatGPT) and open-source models of the Gesellschaft für wissenschaftliche Datenverarbeitung mbH Göttingen (GWDG).

2.1 Access Data / Server Log Files

When accessing GPTalk, the web server of the University of Hohenheim processes the following data:

  • Browser type and language
  • Operating system used
  • Referrer URL (previously visited page)
  • Date and time of the server request
  • Transferred data volume and access status

The collection and storage of this data serve system security and the provision of the service and are automatically deleted after 8 days.

2.2 Authentication and Access Control

Login is carried out via the central Hohenheim user ID. The login credentials are used exclusively for authentication checks and are not stored.

2.3 Storage of Chat Histories

GPTalk 2 enables the storage of individual and group chats to support continuous use and collaborative work processes. The chat history data is stored exclusively on servers of the University of Hohenheim.

  • Purpose of storage: Improving usability, resuming conversations, collaboration in group rooms, and documentation of work processes.
  • Stored personal data: User ID, name, email address, group affiliation (e.g. student, employee), timestamps of messages, as well as metadata (e.g. selected model, system prompts). The contents of the chats (prompts & responses) are stored in encrypted form and are not readable by the server operator.
  • Access rights: Only authorized users have access to their own individual chats and group rooms to which they belong. Authorization is granted via a private key that is stored exclusively locally on the user’s device.
  • Deletion: Users can delete their stored individual and group chats independently.

2.4 Uploaded Files

GPTalk allows file uploads for AI-supported processing. Supported formats:

  • Images: jpg, jpeg, png
  • Documents: pdf, doc, docx, pptx, txt, md, csv, xlsx, xls
  • Code: js, mjs, jsx, ts, tsx, css, json, yml, yaml, xml, php, py, sh, sql

Maximum file size: 10 MB.

  • Storage location: Uploaded files are stored exclusively on servers of the University of Hohenheim.
  • Processing: Documents are converted by GWDG depending on the file format. The GWDG data protection regulations apply; content is not stored by GWDG.
  • Storage duration: Uploaded files are automatically deleted after one month at the latest.
  • Purpose limitation: Files are processed solely within the scope of the user's own use and are not shared with third parties.

3. Use of External AI Services via GPTalk

GPTalk enables access to AI language models from external providers. Depending on the provider, different conditions apply:

3.1 No Disclosure of Personal Data

  • No IP addresses or user IDs are transmitted to external providers.

3.2 Use of GWDG Models (Open Source)

  • Processing takes place on servers of GWDG.
  • Contents (prompts and responses) are not stored.
  • Only time and length of the request/output are recorded for billing purposes – without content reference.

3.3 Use of ChatGPT Models (OpenAI)

  • The contents of the requests (prompts and responses) are stored by OpenAI outside the EU for a maximum of 30 days, solely for the purpose of detecting and preventing misuse.
  • After that, the data is automatically deleted.
  • The contents are not used for training purposes.

3.4 Use of Claude Models (Anthropic)

  • The contents of the requests (prompts and responses) are stored by Anthropic outside the EU for a maximum of 30 days, solely for the purpose of detecting and preventing misuse.
  • After that, the data is automatically deleted.
  • The contents are not used for training purposes.

3.5 Web Search (GPT 4.1 / GPT 5.4 mini)

  • GPTalk offers a web search feature based on the OpenAI models GPT 4.1 and GPT 5.4 mini.
  • When web search is active, requests are transmitted to OpenAI for processing. The same conditions apply as under 3.3 (max. 30 days storage, no use for training purposes).
  • No IP addresses or user IDs are transmitted.

4. Use of Cookies

GPTalk uses technically necessary session cookies. These are automatically deleted when the browser is closed.

5. Your Rights as a User

According to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR) – Insight into the data stored about you
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) – Correction of incorrect data
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) – Deletion of personal data under certain conditions
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Objection (Art. 21 GDPR)

To exercise your rights, please contact the KIM of the University of Hohenheim: gptalk@uni-hohenheim.de
